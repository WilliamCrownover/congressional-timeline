🏛️ Congressional Portrait Downloader

Instructions:

  1. Load the congressional data
  2. The tool will check which portraits are already in your images/ folder
  3. Browse portraits that are available online but not yet downloaded
  4. Click "Download" on any portrait you want to save locally
  5. Downloaded images will be saved to your Downloads folder
  6. Manually move them from Downloads to the images/ folder in your project
