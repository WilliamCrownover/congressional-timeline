🏛️ Congressional Portrait Downloader

Instructions:

  1. Load the congressional data
  2. The tool will check the "congressional-timeline-missing-images" localStorage array
  3. All images NOT in that array are assumed to already exist in your images/ folder
  4. Browse portraits that are missing and available online
  5. Click "Download" on any portrait you want to save locally
  6. Successfully downloaded images are automatically removed from the missing list
  7. Downloaded images will be saved to your Downloads folder
  8. Manually move them from Downloads to the images/ folder in your project
